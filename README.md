# Welcome

Welcome to the **CATALONIA-CERT Forensics Documentation**.

This repository is designed as a practical reference for security analysts, incident responders, and digital forensics teams. Its primary objective is to systematically **document the artifacts, traces and indicators** left behind by tools, techniques, and procedures commonly observed during security incidents and intrusions.

The content is organized to provide structured, repeatable, and actionable information that supports detection, investigation, and post-incident analysis.

Within this documentation you will find:

* System artifacts (registry keys, files, services, logs, etc.).
* Execution and configuration traces.
* Observable indicators on endpoints and networks.
* Platform-specific considerations for Windows and Linux systems.

This guide is not intended to explain how these tools operate from an offensive standpoint. Instead, it focuses exclusively on **how to identify them and understand their footprint from a defensive and investigative perspective**.

# Who are we?

## Incident Response Team of CATALONIA-CERT

The **CATALONIA-CERT** is the cybersecurity incident response center for Catalonia. Its purpose is to be a reliable and trusted point of contact for reporting, communicating, and managing cybersecurity incidents. The team operates under the *Agència de Ciberseguretat de Catalunya* and serves as the region’s official Computer Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT) for the Government of Catalonia.

Our mission is to provide coordinated cybersecurity incident handling, working with public institutions, local administrations, healthcare, universities, businesses, and citizens. We implement internationally recognized best practices for incident management, following models such as those defined by the Software Engineering Institute (SEI) at Carnegie Mellon University.

CATALONIA-CERT is an official member of FIRST, the global Forum of Incident Response and Security Teams, connecting with over 350 teams worldwide to share insights, warnings, and collaboration on threats. We are also accredited by the Trusted Introducer for CERTs in Europe and members of the CSIRT Network.
